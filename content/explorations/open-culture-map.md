A continent-wide map of cultural heritage: museums, archaeological sites,
monuments and artworks pulled from OpenStreetMap and enriched with the
identifiers, descriptions and links that Wikidata carries for each one.

Like its sibling editions it refreshes weekly, so the picture keeps pace with
the open datasets underneath it. See also
[Open Wine Map](/explorations/open-wine-map/) and
[Open Art Map](/explorations/open-art-map/).
