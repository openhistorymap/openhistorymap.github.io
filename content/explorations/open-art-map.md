An atlas built around three places in every artist's life: where they were
born, where they died, and where their work hangs today. Tracing those points
turns the history of art into a geography — movements migrating, careers
crossing borders, collections gathering far from where they were made.

Assembled from Wikidata for the people and the works and OpenStreetMap for the
institutions that hold them, and refreshed monthly. A companion to
[Open Culture Map](/explorations/open-culture-map/).
