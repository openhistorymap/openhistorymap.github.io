A living atlas of wine: every winery and vineyard that OpenStreetMap records,
set against the regions and appellations described in Wikidata, so you can see
both the individual estates and the named territories they sit inside.

The whole map rebuilds itself weekly, so it tracks the open data as it grows
rather than freezing a single snapshot. It is one of the present-day,
OSM-plus-Wikidata editions alongside
[Open Culture Map](/explorations/open-culture-map/) and
[Open Art Map](/explorations/open-art-map/).
