Photography reaches back only to the late 18th century, but people have been
making located views of the world for far longer. Visions of the Past gathers
those views — photographs, paintings, engravings — and lets you walk among
them on a map, choosing both *where* you stand and *when*.

It is the public face of the Historical Street View project; the back-end that
serves the located views, [`hsv-api`](/tools/), is listed under Tools.
