The Great War rendered day by day: move along the timeline and watch the
fronts shift, the battles open and close, and the bombing runs fall, each
event placed in space and pinned to its date.

It draws on the THOR bombing dataset, Wikidata for the events and actors, and
OpenStreetMap for the underlying geography. It is the elder of the two war
atlases — see also
[Map of the Second World War](/explorations/map-of-the-second-world-war/).
