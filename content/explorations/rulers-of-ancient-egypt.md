The pharaohs, dynasty by dynasty: from Narmer and the unification of the Two
Lands through the Old, Middle and New Kingdoms to Cleopatra VII and the Roman
annexation of Egypt.

Built on Wikipedia and Wikidata, it is the direct sibling of
[Rulers of Ancient Rome](/explorations/rulers-of-ancient-rome/) — the same
portrait-gallery treatment applied to one of the longest continuous lines of
rulership in recorded history.
