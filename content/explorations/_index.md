Alongside the core map, the OHM team builds focused, public-facing editions:
each takes a single subject — the rulers of an age, the ideas of a
civilisation, the wineries of a region, the front lines of a war — and renders
it as its own atlas, gallery or timeline.

They share the platform's machinery (time-aware tiles, the data index, the
located-views back-end) but each stands on its own domain, with its own
audience. Most are assembled from open sources — Wikidata, Wikipedia,
OpenStreetMap and, for the war atlases, the THOR dataset — and several refresh
themselves on a schedule.
