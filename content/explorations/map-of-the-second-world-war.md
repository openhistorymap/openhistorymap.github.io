The same day-by-day treatment applied to the Second World War: the war's reach
unfolding across the timeline, with the THOR dataset — the US Department of
Defense's record of aerial bombardment — supplying the operational detail and
Wikidata and OpenStreetMap supplying the events and the ground beneath them.

It is the newer of the pair and still settling in; the
[Map of the First World War](/explorations/map-of-the-first-world-war/) is the
more finished sibling.
