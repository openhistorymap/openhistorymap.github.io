Most lists of rulers run *down* a single throne. This one runs *across*: pick a
year and see who was in power everywhere at once — the emperor in Rome, the
dynasty in China, the king in a European fragment — laid side by side on one
synchronic timeline.

It is assembled from Wikipedia and Wikidata and covers antiquity through the
18th century. The same engine drives the focused sibling editions for
[Ancient Rome](/explorations/rulers-of-ancient-rome/) and
[Ancient Egypt](/explorations/rulers-of-ancient-egypt/).
