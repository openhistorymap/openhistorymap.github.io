Rome is usually told through its rulers and its wars. This edition tells it
through its *minds*: the engineers, jurists, poets and philosophers, and the
inventions and ideas they left behind — concrete and aqueducts, law and
calendar, rhetoric and road.

Laid out as a single timeline from the traditional founding of the city in
753 BC to the fall of the West in AD 476, and built on Wikidata and Wikipedia.
It is the conceptual companion to the
[Rulers of Ancient Rome](/explorations/rulers-of-ancient-rome/) gallery.
