The reading side of the data index: a browser interface for searching the
sources, datasets and `ohm:*` descriptors that the
[Index API](/tools/ohm-index-api/) serves.

It is where a researcher goes to ask *what does OHM know about this place,
this period, this theme* — and to trace any answer back to the Zotero item it
came from.
