Open Culture Map

A weekly-refreshed cultural map of Europe — museums, archaeological sites, artworks and heritage from OpenStreetMap, enriched with Wikidata.

openhistorymap.org/openculturemap

A continent-wide map of cultural heritage: museums, archaeological sites, monuments and artworks pulled from OpenStreetMap and enriched with the identifiers, descriptions and links that Wikidata carries for each one.

Like its sibling editions it refreshes weekly, so the picture keeps pace with the open datasets underneath it. See also Open Wine Map and Open Art Map.
